# Word games

**Problem.** Learners needed a reason to open the app on days they did not feel like speaking. Games had to work without a microphone.

**What I built.**
- Four two-player English games, including a real-time multiplayer word hunt on a 150k-word dictionary.
- **Wend**, a daily word-path puzzle with 365 levels and streaks.

**Notes.**
- Real-time play is synced between two phones, with a lobby and reconnect handling.
- Every level in the daily puzzle is generated and then checked to be solvable.
- The day rolls over on Indian time, so streaks do not break at odd hours.
